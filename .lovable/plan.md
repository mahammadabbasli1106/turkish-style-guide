
# Font + Dashboard Redesign: Satoshi Font, Weather Bar, and "The Daily Edit"

Three changes that will transform the dashboard from informational to editorial.

---

## 1. Switch to Satoshi Font Globally

Replace the current Playfair Display (headings) and Inter (body) fonts with Satoshi everywhere for a clean, modern, minimalist aesthetic.

- Update `src/index.css` to import Satoshi from a CDN (fontsource or similar) instead of Playfair Display + Inter
- Update CSS custom properties `--font-display` and `--font-body` to both use Satoshi
- Update `tailwind.config.ts` font families to reference Satoshi

---

## 2. Minimalist Weather Bar

Replace the large purple gradient weather card with a slim, single-line horizontal bar.

**Design:** A compact row showing:
`Ankara . 9deg Overcast . "Perfect for a light trench coat today."`

The style tip portion will be generated by mapping weather conditions to short clothing recommendations (e.g., rain = "Grab an umbrella and waterproof jacket", clear + warm = "Light layers are your friend today").

**Changes:**
- Rewrite `src/components/dashboard/WeatherWidget.tsx` to render a thin horizontal bar instead of the big card
- Remove the `WeatherEffects` overlay (no longer needed on a thin bar)
- Move weather from its current position to right below the greeting in `Dashboard.tsx`
- Update `DashboardSkeleton.tsx` to match the new slim weather bar shape

---

## 3. "The Daily Edit" -- Style Recommendation Hero

Replace the current `OutfitOfTheDay` card with a premium editorial-style hero section. This becomes the visual centerpiece of the dashboard.

**When outfit exists:**
- Large card with a subtle gradient background
- Day-aware title: "Your Monday Vibe: [style from latest outfit]" (e.g., "Your Monday Vibe: Refined Minimalist")
- The AI reasoning as a short subtitle
- Clothing item thumbnails in a horizontal strip
- Tappable, links to outfit history

**When no outfit exists:**
- Stylish CTA card with editorial copy: "What's your vibe today?" with a button to get a suggestion

**Changes:**
- Rewrite `src/components/dashboard/OutfitOfTheDay.tsx` with the new "Daily Edit" design
- Add new i18n keys for day-aware titles and copy in `src/lib/i18n.ts`

---

## New Dashboard Order

After changes, the dashboard layout becomes:

1. Greeting + date
2. Weather bar (slim, inline)
3. The Daily Edit hero card
4. Quick Actions
5. Stats Row
6. Action Cards
7. Daily Tip
8. Getting Started banner

---
